## Brand & Style

This design system embodies institutional precision, financial resilience, and sovereign infrastructure for enterprise CFOs and treasury leaders. The aesthetic sits at the intersection of classical editorial authority and ultra-clean modern corporate fintech: stable, measured, and uncompromisingly professional.

### Audience & Mood
The experience caters to global finance teams, enterprise treasurers, and corporate controllers who demand auditability, speed, and absolute clarity. The visual language evokes reliability, technical finality, and institutional weight without falling into legacy banking stagnation. Every element projects security, speed of settlement, and deterministic outcome control.

### Design Movement: Classical Modern Corporate
- **Structured Editorial Headings:** Authoritative serif titles bring the dignity of long-standing financial institutions.
- **Engineered Utilitarian Controls:** High-legibility geometric sans-serif interfaces paired with monospaced infrastructure indicators for payment rails, clearing codes, and currency amounts.
- **Disciplined Chromatic Hierarchy:** High restraint across vast neutral surfaces, using surgical placements of orange for definitive singular conversion paths and cyan for data connectivity and institutional anchors.

## Colors

The palette establishes strict behavioral rules to prevent chromatic clutter in complex financial views.

### Action & Brand Colors
- **Brand Orange (`#D87808`):** Reserved strictly for primary call-to-action triggers and critical affirmative financial actions (e.g., "Approve Batch", "Execute Disbursement"). Exactly one primary orange button is permitted per section view. Supporting shades: Hover `#BE6906`, Light `#E89A40`, Tint `#FBEAD2`. Never use Brand Orange for informational badges or decorative backgrounds.
- **Brand Cyan (`#009DC2`):** The connectivity and interactive anchor. Used for hyperlinks, active tab indicators, data visualizations, focused states, and selective single-word accents inside editorial headlines. It must never fill a primary CTA button. Supporting shades: Hover `#008AAB`, Deep `#007387`, Light `#4FC0D9`, Tint `#CBEAF2`, Wash `#E9F6FA`.
- **Sky-Cyan Band (`#88D0E0`):** An alternating background wash used across large-scale data bands or trust breakout modules, paired strictly with Ink text. Lighter variant: `#A9DEEA`.

### Base & Surface Palette
- **Ink (`#132230`):** The foundational dark tone for body copy, primary headings, tabular values, and the logotype wordmark.
- **Slate Stack:** Dark institutional surfaces. Deep Dark `#283038`, Slate Base `#303840`, Lifted Slate `#3A444E`. Used for dark mode modules, code panels, and contrasting summary headers.
- **Neutral Slate Scale:**
  - `n-50`: `#F6F8FA` (Canvas background)
  - `n-100`: `#EDF1F5` (Subtle card base & table headers)
  - `n-200`: `#E2E7ED` (Hairline dividers, inactive borders)
  - `n-300`: `#CBD5E1` (Input borders, control outlines)
  - `n-500`: `#64748B` (Secondary text, metadata, table column headers)
  - `n-700`: `#334155` (Emphasized secondary text)
  - `n-900`: `#132230` (Ink / highest contrast)

### Semantic & Ledger States
Semantic indicators use muted tinted backgrounds paired with deep high-contrast text:
- **Success:** `#1F9D6B` on `#E4F4ED` (Settled, Reconciled, Verified)
- **Warning:** `#D98A0B` on `#FBF0DC` (Escrow Hold, Compliance Review)
- **Danger:** `#D6463C` on `#FAE7E5` (Failed Rail, Revoked Access, KYC Rejection)
- **Info:** Brand Cyan `#009DC2` on `#E9F6FA` (Processing, Informational Status)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines literary authority with corporate fintech agility.

### Structural Typographic Rules
- **Display and Title Levels:** Built with Source Serif 4 (an authoritative, highly structured serif alternative delivering exact editorial weight). Headings feature tight vertical leading and slight negative tracking.
- **Selective Accent Words:** In H1 and H2 lockups, exactly one high-impact operational word per headline may be color-coded with Brand Cyan (`#009DC2`) to guide parsing without eroding editorial dignity.
- **Action, Navigation, and Badges:** Montserrat handles all functional utility copy. Primary buttons, secondary actions, global navigation items, eyebrow tags, table metadata headers, and logotype wordmarks are set strictly to `UPPERCASE` with wide letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).
- **Financial Monospace Engine:** JetBrains Mono is assigned to all clearing identifiers, currency values, ledger logs, bank rails (e.g., `US · ACH`, `EU · SEPA`, `SWIFT · BIC`), routing numbers, and cryptographic settlement receipts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column responsive fluid grid built on an 8pt architectural rhythm, with a supplementary 4pt sub-grid for dense financial data structures and tables.

### Breakpoints & Containers
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max container width `1280px`, `24px` gutter, `48px` outer page margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, fluid width, `16px` gutter, `24px` outer page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, fluid width, `12px` gutter, `16px` outer page margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`2px` – `12px`):** Used inside form controls, badge padding, rail identifiers, and icon-to-label gaps.
- **Component Spacing (`16px` – `32px`):** Used within cards, modal dialogs, data table cell padding, and between related inputs.
- **Section Spacing (`64px` – `96px`):** Defines large departmental divisions, narrative marketing bands, and major ledger transitions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via structural hairline outlines and diffused ambient lighting. Floating gimmicks, heavy drop-shadows, and colored accent borders are strictly forbidden.

### Tonal Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F6F8FA` in light mode, or `#283038` in dark modular viewports.
- **Tier 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** Flat `#FFFFFF` or lifted dark `#3A444E`, bound by a 1px solid hairline border in `#E2E7ED` (or `#303840` in dark surfaces).
- **Tier 2 (Popovers, Dropdowns, Date Pickers):** Elevated surface with hairline border accompanied by ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(19, 34, 48, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(19, 34, 48, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Settlement Confirmations):** Centered overlay surface supported by an ink backcloth (`rgba(19, 34, 48, 0.65)`) with a deep ambient shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(19, 34, 48, 0.16), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(19, 34, 48, 0.08)`.

### Border Integrity
Cards, list rows, and data summaries rely on razor-sharp 1px neutral hairline dividers rather than elevated drop shadows. Never apply colored left-border strips to cards or status boxes.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional, engineered shape discipline:

- **Interactive Primary & Secondary Buttons:** Fixed at `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius. This creates crisp, professional controls without appearing unrefined or overly casual.
- **Cards and Data Shells:** Set to `10px` or `12px` corner radius. Clean, structural enclosures that preserve maximum inner real estate for dense financial data tables.
- **Status Badges & Rail Tags:** Finished with `4px` or full pill curvature (`9999px`) strictly when demarcating live payment rails and transaction statuses.
- **Input Fields:** Form controls share the exact `8px` radius of action buttons to ensure geometric consistency across transaction approval rows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid brand orange (`#D87808`) fill, `#FFFFFF` uppercase bold typography (`label-caps`), `8px` corner radius, `12px 24px` padding. Subtle hover state: `#BE6906`. Active state: `#A35804`. Focused state: 2px offset outline using Brand Cyan (`#009DC2`). Exactly one primary button per screen context.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px hairline border in `#CBD5E1` (or translucent white border on dark bands), `#132230` uppercase bold typography (`label-caps`), `8px` corner radius. Hover state: `#F6F8FA` background with `#132230` border.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button:** Transparent background, Brand Cyan (`#009DC2`) uppercase bold typography. Hover: `#E9F6FA` background.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Height: `44px` standard. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Radius: `8px`.
- Typography: Montserrat `15px` (`body-md`) with `#132230` text. Placeholder: `#64748B`.
- Focus State: 1px border `#009DC2` and subtle 3px focus ring `rgba(0, 157, 194, 0.15)`.
- Numeric & Account Inputs: Displayed in `JetBrains Mono` for rapid scanning and digit alignment.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox: `18px x 18px`, `4px` radius. Neutral state: 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: `#009DC2` fill with white checkmark icon.
- Radio: `18px x 18px`, circular. Checked state: `#009DC2` ring with `#009DC2` centered dot.

### Cards & Ledger Containers
- Background: Surface white (`#FFFFFF`) or lifted slate (`#3A444E`) for dark contexts.
- Border: 1px neutral hairline border (`#E2E7ED`).
- Radius: `12px`.
- Elevation: Ambient diffuse shadow. No colored vertical indicator strips on left borders. Status must be communicated via badges and chips within the card body.

### Payment Rail Tags & Status Badges
- **Rail Tags (e.g., `US · ACH`, `EU · SEPA`):** Background `#F6F8FA`, border 1px solid `#E2E7ED`, text JetBrains Mono `11px` (`code-mono-sm`), `#334155` color, `4px` radius, padding `2px 6px`.
- **Status Badges:** Text `11px` bold uppercase (`label-eyebrow`), padding `4px 8px`, `4px` or pill radius.
  - *Settled:* Text `#1F9D6B`, background `#E4F4ED`.
  - *Hold:* Text `#D98A0B`, background `#FBF0DC`.
  - *Failed:* Text `#D6463C`, background `#FAE7E5`.
  - *Pending:* Text `#009DC2`, background `#E9F6FA`.

### Financial Data Tables
- Header Row: Background `#F6F8FA`, uppercase `11px` Montserrat tracking `0.08em`, color `#64748B`, 1px hairline border-bottom `#E2E7ED`.
- Table Rows: Alternating hover state `#F6F8FA`, height `52px`, vertical alignment center.
- Cell Values: Monetary values and clearing hashes aligned right using `JetBrains Mono`. Descriptions and payee names aligned left using Montserrat.
